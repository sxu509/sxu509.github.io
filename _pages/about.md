I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Genetics and Genome Sciences at **UConn Health**, working with Dr. Dongyuan Song. I develop statistical and computational methods for single-cell and spatial omics, with a focus on spatial alignment, multimodal integration, deconvolution, and the analysis of tissue organization.

I received my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics, with a concentration in Statistics, from the **University of North Carolina at Charlotte** in May 2025, advised by Dr. Shaoyu Li and Dr. Duan Chen. My doctoral research focused on statistical methods for the deconvolution of bulk RNA-seq data, including matrix factorization and the evaluation of computational methods.

My research brings together statistics, machine learning, and computational biology. I am interested in building interpretable and reproducible tools that integrate molecular, spatial, and imaging data to understand tissue structure and cellular heterogeneity.

You can find my work on the [Research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) page.
