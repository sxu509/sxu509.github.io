I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Genetics and Genome Sciences at **UConn Health**, working with Dr. Dongyuan Song. I develop statistical and computational methods for single-cell and spatial omics, focusing on spatial alignment and multimodal integration. My work combines molecular profiles with spatial and imaging data to study tissue organization and cellular heterogeneity, with an emphasis on rigorous benchmarking and reproducible, open-source software.

I received my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics, with a concentration in Statistics, from the **University of North Carolina at Charlotte** in May 2025, advised by Dr. Shaoyu Li and Dr. Duan Chen. My doctoral research focused on developing matrix factorization methods for bulk RNA-seq deconvolution and systematically evaluating the accuracy and robustness of deconvolution methods.

You can find my publications and ongoing projects on the [Research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) page.
