## Courses

I served as an Instructor of Record at the **University of North Carolina at Charlotte** (2021–2025), teaching more than 300 students across four semesters.

- **STAT 1220** — Elements of Statistics I
- **STAT 2122** — Introduction to Probability and Statistics
- **STAT 2223** — Elements of Statistics II

My teaching covers probability, statistical inference, regression, and applied data analysis, with R- and Python-based activities.
